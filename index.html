NHL SOG Rankings

NHL SOG Rankings is an owner-operated personal research tool that estimates NHL skaters' probability of recording at least one shot on goal during the first period. It uses public hockey data and displays experimental rankings and availability context in the operator's private Google Sheet.

Current status

The project is in experimental quality assurance. Cloud publishing and recovery have been tested; automatic scheduling is not yet installed. Predictions are not prospectively validated and do not promise betting profit.

The application does not log in to sportsbook accounts, prepare tickets, transfer funds, or place bets. The operator makes every betting decision and places any bets manually.

Why Google access is requested

The app reads and updates its designated project spreadsheet and preserves project caches, source evidence, forecast history, and recovery backups in private Google Drive files. Deterministic processing runs through GitHub Actions. A small Google Apps Script timing coordinator is planned, subject to authorization, free-resource controls, and operational checks.

Private application

This site provides public application and privacy information. It has no registration or Google sign-in and does not expose the private Sheet, rankings, research records, or credentials.

Terms of use

This is a personal research project, not a public betting or account-registration service. Information is experimental and supplied without a guarantee of accuracy, availability, or financial return. Use of Google and GitHub remains subject to their respective terms. This site does not provide a betting service.
